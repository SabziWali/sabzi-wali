How to Store Leafy Greens So They Stay Fresh Longer

Leafy greens such as spinach, lettuce, coriander and other fresh vegetables can wilt quickly if they are stored incorrectly. With proper moisture control and refrigeration, you can help keep leafy greens fresh and usable for longer.

1. Remove Damaged Leaves

Before storing leafy greens, check them carefully and remove leaves that are already damaged, slimy or badly wilted. Keeping healthier leaves separate can help maintain their quality.

2. Keep Leafy Greens Dry

Excess water left on leaves can encourage spoilage. If the greens have been washed, dry them thoroughly before storing them.

Quick tip: A clean paper towel can help absorb excess moisture around leafy greens during storage.

3. Store Them in the Refrigerator

Refrigeration is generally the best option for keeping leafy greens fresh at home. Store them in a suitable part of the refrigerator where they remain cool without being crushed.

4. Use a Bag or Container

Place leafy greens in a clean food-storage bag or suitable container. Avoid packing them so tightly that the leaves become crushed.

5. Use Paper Towels to Control Moisture

Place a clean paper towel around or alongside the greens to absorb excess moisture. Replace the towel if it becomes noticeably wet.

6. Do Not Store Damaged Greens With Fresh Greens

Check stored greens regularly. Remove leaves that have become slimy, moldy or significantly spoiled so they do not affect the remaining vegetables.

7. How Long Do Leafy Greens Last?

Storage life depends on the type of leafy green, its freshness when purchased and the storage conditions. Proper refrigeration and moisture control can help extend freshness.

How to Tell If Leafy Greens Have Gone Bad

Signs of spoilage can include a strong unpleasant smell, slimy texture, visible mold or significant discoloration and deterioration. Greens showing clear signs of spoilage should not be used.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should leafy greens be refrigerated?

Yes. Refrigeration is generally the best way to slow wilting and help keep leafy greens fresh for longer.

Should I wash leafy greens before storing them?

If possible, store leafy greens dry and wash them shortly before use. If you wash them before storage, dry them thoroughly first.

Why do leafy greens become wilted?

Leafy greens can lose water during storage, which causes them to become limp or wilted. Cool storage and suitable moisture control can help reduce wilting.
