How to Store Carrots So They Last Longer

Carrots are easy to store, but the wrong storage conditions can make them soft, dry or spoiled sooner than expected. With a few simple steps, you can help carrots stay fresh and crisp for longer at home.

1. Remove the Green Tops

If your carrots still have green tops attached, remove the tops before storing them. The leafy tops can draw moisture from the carrot and may cause the carrots to lose their firmness more quickly.

2. Do Not Wash Carrots Before Long-Term Storage

If you are storing carrots for several days or longer, it is generally better to keep them dry and wash them shortly before use. Excess moisture can encourage spoilage.

3. Keep Carrots in the Refrigerator

For most homes, the refrigerator is a practical place to store carrots. Keep them in the vegetable or crisper section where they can stay cool without being exposed to excessive moisture.

4. Use a Bag or Container

Place carrots in a suitable food-storage bag or container. If there is excess moisture inside, use a clean paper towel to help absorb it. Avoid sealing visibly wet carrots in a way that traps water.

Quick tip: Check stored carrots every few days and remove any carrot that has started to spoil so the problem does not spread to the others.

5. Keep Carrots Away From Ethylene-Producing Produce

Some fruits and vegetables release ethylene gas as they ripen. Keeping carrots separated from strongly ethylene-producing produce can help maintain their quality during storage.

6. How Long Do Carrots Last?

Storage life depends on the freshness of the carrots, temperature, humidity and storage conditions. Proper refrigeration can help fresh carrots remain usable considerably longer than leaving them at room temperature.

7. How to Tell If Carrots Have Gone Bad

Check carrots before using them. Very soft texture, an unpleasant smell, visible mold or significant deterioration are signs that the carrot should not be used.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should carrots be refrigerated?

Yes. Refrigeration is generally the most practical way to keep fresh carrots in good condition for longer.

Should I wash carrots before storing them?

For longer storage, keeping them dry and washing them shortly before use can help reduce excess moisture during storage.

Why do carrots become soft?

Carrots can lose moisture during storage, which may make them soft or less crisp. Proper refrigeration and suitable storage can help reduce moisture loss.
